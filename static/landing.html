The parts that make it a job

There's no answer key, because the work either got done or it didn't. What you get is the people, the deadlines, and the consequences.

Reviews check the evidence

Your manager can't approve work that doesn't exist. An "approved" means the email was sent, the ledger was fixed, the ticket was closed.

Colleagues with their own agenda

A teammate who's sometimes wrong on purpose. A client who escalates when ignored. HR who reads your tone. They remember what you did.

Questions

Is this a course or a quiz?

It's a job. You work inside the tools the role uses, and a manager grades the output. There's nothing to watch and nothing to memorise.

Do I need an account to try it?

Guest mode starts a simulation straight from the browser. Make an account when you want your score and progress to stick.

Can it simulate the exact job I'm applying for?

Paste the job description and the engine builds a simulation around it: the company, the colleagues, the tasks, and the rubric your work gets graded on.

What does the employability score measure?

Approved tasks, review outcomes, and interview results. Every point traces back to work the system checked.

Will employers take the score seriously?

It carries more than a certificate because there's a trail of graded work behind it. You can walk an interviewer through what you did, day by day. It won't replace experience, and it isn't meant to.

I'm switching careers. Which desk do I pick?

Pick the track closest to the role you're targeting. If you already have the job ad, paste it and skip the catalogue entirely.

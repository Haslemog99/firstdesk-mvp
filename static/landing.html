How it works

Three steps. Then the day starts.

You sign in, pick the work, and spend a compressed office day at the desk. The simulator keeps the inbox, the tasks, and the score.

  1. 01

    Choose the work

    Pick one of eight career tracks, or paste a public job listing or the description itself. Then say how familiar you are. The job stays the same. Time, explanations, and how hard mistakes bite do not.

    • Beginner · 2h 30m
    • Studied · 2h 00m
    • Experienced · 1h 30m
  2. 02

    Work the day

    The desk clock runs 09:00 to 17:00. Read the inbox, answer in Slack, use the role’s tools, and show up to a timed meeting. Coworkers move deadlines. IT outages and tool downtime can interrupt you. The clock pauses after three minutes without an action.

  3. 03

    Read the score

    End the day for a review out of 100, a letter grade, and manager notes. Come back for the next day. Each track runs five simulated days, and a registered account keeps your best score per career.

Features

A desk, not a quiz.

Everything below is in the simulator today. Outlook, Slack, and the role tools are in-simulation. They do not connect to a real mailbox or workspace.

  • Outlook-style inbox

    Read, reply, forward, Cc and Bcc, and file mail into folders. High-priority messages are marked. Actionable mail is part of the score.

  • Slack-style chat

    Channels follow the career — #finance, #soc-alerts, #incidents, #marketing, #partnerships, #engineering — plus #general. You can read and post.

  • The tools of the role

    Sage and sheets, a SIEM and Jira, campaigns and analytics, CI, purchase orders, an LMS, a roadmap, a risk register. Tasks, an HR checklist, training, IT setup, and files sit alongside them.

  • AI coworkers

    A manager, a client, a teammate, IT, HR, and a director. They email you and each other. You see the thread when you are copied. On harder settings they stop explaining and let a mistake land.

  • Consequences

    Actions ripple. A wrong ledger entry, a false escalation, or a late client reply can come back as mail, a Slack message, and a note on your conduct standing.

  • Your own job description

    Paste a Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, Ashby, Indeed, Reed, or Wellfound URL, or the text. The scenario engine writes a fresh day for accounting, cybersecurity, marketing, and software roles.

  • Interview practice

    A separate multi-round mock interview scores content, delivery, and confidence. One mode runs after a simulation and asks you to defend the day you just worked. It is marked Pro inside the app.

Who it’s for

People about to be new.

The same desk, three reasons to sit at it.

Graduates

Beginner mode is built for a first job you have not done yet. You get a study guide, hints, and tool explainers. Colleagues warn you before a serious mistake, the first slip on each tool is free, and the office day takes two and a half hours of real time.

Career changers

Start a track you have not worked, or paste a description of the role you are moving toward. Studied mode assumes the theory and scores you straight. Experienced mode drops the explanations and makes penalties one and a half times as sharp.

Job seekers

Paste the listing you are applying to. For accounting, cybersecurity, marketing, and software roles, the scenario engine builds a day from it. Interview practice, marked Pro in the app, can follow so you talk through what you actually did.

FAQ

Short answers.

How long is a simulated day?

On the desk, the day runs from 09:00 to 17:00. In real time that is 2 hours 30 minutes on beginner, 2 hours on studied, and 1 hour 30 minutes on experienced. If you leave the tab idle for three minutes, the clock pauses instead of burning the day.

What am I scored on?

Task execution (25), communication (20), prioritisation (20), independence (15), pushback and judgment (10), and speed and efficiency (10). The total is out of 100, with a letter grade from A+ to D and notes from the manager. Registered accounts also see an employability figure on the dashboard, taken from the best simulation score and, if you have done one, the best interview score.

Which careers can be generated from a job description?

The scenario engine’s careers are accountant, cybersecurity, marketing, and developer. Those four have a tool palette the generator is allowed to use. Procurement, learning and development, product management, and GRC are full five-day simulations you start from the career list. A pasted listing can be auto-classified onto any of the eight tracks; generation of a new day only proceeds when the track has a palette.

Do I need an account?

You can try a day as a guest from the sign-in screen. Guest progress is not saved permanently. An account keeps your simulation, your scores, and a way back into an unfinished day. You can delete the account and its data from the dashboard.

Does this connect to my real email or Slack?

No. The inbox, chat, and role tools are simulated. Messages stay inside your FirstDesk session. Password reset is the only email the app sends, and only for your own account.

What does interview practice cost?

Mock interviews and the simulate-then-interview path are marked Pro inside the app. They score content, delivery, and confidence across rounds.

Pricing placeholder — the product does not publish a price. Nothing on this page is a fee, plan, or offer.

Can I stop halfway?

A registered session remembers the career, the day, and the difficulty. The dashboard offers to continue an in-progress day. Signing out and back in returns you to that desk rather than wiping it.

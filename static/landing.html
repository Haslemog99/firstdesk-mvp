A whole company that exists to test you

Every simulation spins up a believable company around you — inbox, chat, files, deadlines — and colleagues with their own personalities, agendas, and standards.

AI colleagues with real personas

A manager who assigns and reviews your work. A teammate who pushes back — sometimes wrongly, to test you. HR, IT, the client, the director. They remember what you did.

Evidence-graded reviews

Your manager can't approve work you didn't do. Approvals check the artifacts you produced — the email sent, the ledger fixed, the ticket closed. Score work the way a real boss does.

Real tools, not slides

Outlook, team chat, Jira-style boards, spreadsheets, ledgers, a SIEM with EDR telemetry — the tools each role actually uses, on a desktop that feels like day one at the office.

Workplace physics

Ignore the client and they escalate to your manager. Miss a deadline and HR notices. Every action ripples — a quiet inbox is its own signal. The sim reacts like a real org.

Mock interviews that listen

Voice-based AI interviews scored on what you say and how you say it — using prosody and body-language signals, not a keyword checklist. Combo mode chains a sim straight into the interview.

Questions

Is this a course or a quiz?

Neither. There are no lectures and no multiple-choice questions. You do the actual work of the role inside simulated tools, and an AI manager grades the artifacts you produce.

Can it simulate the exact job I'm applying for?

Yes — paste a job description and the scenario engine builds a bespoke simulation around that role's responsibilities, tools, and grading rubric.

What does the employability score measure?

Points earned across approved tasks, review outcomes, and interview performance. It reflects work the system verified — not skills you claim.

Who is it for?

Job seekers who want practice and proof before interviews, and L&D / hiring teams who want to assess candidates on real work rather than CVs.
